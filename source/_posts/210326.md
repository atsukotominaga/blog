Finally, spring has come. The weather was really nice today. Tofu went outside more than before. She must be happy, although she doesn't look so amused by this weather in the photo above.

![](/uploads/210326/20260312_115710035.jpg)

She has been observing outside a lot lately... butterflies and bees are coming back. Also, she will probably resume hunting mice and bring them back to us as gifts. She will eat everything except the heart.

![](/uploads/210326/20260321_175156824.jpg)

Tofu likes sitting in our guest room / my work room at sunset. Probably because it is a bit warmer there. She also likes observing people and the neighbourhood cats through this window.

![](/uploads/210326/20260218_212556394.jpg)

A few weeks ago, Tofu was sick. Usually, she eats her meal in one go, but she couldn't finish it. She ate small amounts at a time. It turned out that she had a cat flu. Fortunately, nothing serious, Tofu has fully recovered thanks to the medication.

![](/uploads/210326/20260301_224952856.jpg)

At the moment, she is still sleeping indoors at night but I think she will start going outside once it gets warmer.
